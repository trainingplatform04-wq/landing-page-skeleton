## Scope

The two sections above, 375 / 768 / 1440, compared in English on `/en/about`; German is checked
by E2E and by its own baselines on `/ueber-uns`. The menu gains one entry, "About", after
"Magazine" (`nav.about`); our footer holds only the legal links, so the prototype's footer entry is not built. The home page and every other page are
unchanged, apart from the home about section linking to the new page (our decision, brief 0003,
not part of the prototype).

## Tokens

None. Built with **our current theme**, as the magazine and the article page. The specs name the
prototype's tokens; the frontend maps each to our closest Nuxt UI semantic token and keeps every
measurement: `primary` → `primary`, `muted-foreground` → `text-muted`, `background` → page
background, `surface-strong` (closing band) → `bg-inverted` with `text-inverted`.

  desktop (`webPerformance.config.ts`).

## Deviations

- The prototype's picture is generated by Lovable and is not downloaded: the seed uploads a
  stock photo (Unsplash, as the other demo pictures) of a coach with a client, cropped 21:9.
- The prototype's closing band is its own component with the same markup as the magazine's;
  ours reuses `MagazineClosing` with `section="about-closing"` and the page's own title, text
  and button.
- Between 768 and 1279 the picture is 16:9; from 1280 it is 21:9 (the prototype's `xl`
  breakpoint), which includes the 1440 design width.
- The prototype's `<head>` tags (title, description, `og:*`) come from our SEO fields and the
  site defaults.
